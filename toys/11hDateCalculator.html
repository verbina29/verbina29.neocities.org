eleventh hour's calendar is based on powers of 2 and themed around colors. Its year 0 is the year mana processing was discovered, so it's eras are AM and PM, standing for ante mana and post mana, the same as real life AM and PM but with mana. (They use 24-hour time, so there's no confusion there.). The years are 256 days, with 8 months in a year, 32 days in a month, 4 weeks in a month, and 8 days in a week. Most of the terms here are based on a cycle of colors, with the colors being red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, and "vivid", with vivid generally being a special case. For example, the vivid month has its own cycle through the colors, and the vivid day depends on the week and "colors" the week. Years have a color that syncs up with the vivid month, and there's also a second cycle of terms that goes every 8 years. A lot of the names here are fairly generic/obvious, so I might change some of them around later.

Since the calendar has all these fun color associations, it lends itself well to an astrology-like system. Of course, this is equally as bullshit in-universe as astrology is in real life. The average person in-universe is going to put almost no stock in any of this. Everything here is pretty up-to-interpretation, so the astrology reading part is just my personal interpretation. Coming up with your own interpretations is pretty fun, since it's mostly based on color theory. Most of the astrology info was completely bullshitted on the spot. I was originally going to have it be more of a write-up, but it's actually pretty hard to write out all that info so I switched gears to just brief lists. i did eventually start using a random character trait generator. but i did select ones that i think fit somewhat still okay.

Since the 11h calendar can't really map 1:1 onto the real calendar, some sacrifices have been made. 11h days greater than the number of days in the real month loop around back to 1, and IRL months above 8 alternate between being the 11h months 1-4 and 5-8 based on the year. As such, the mapping can't be 1:1, so converting a date back and forth doesn't always result in the same date. Years are kept the same, but the current year in 11h is 1101.
